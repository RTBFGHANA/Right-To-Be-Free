Donate

Right To Be Free is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization in the USA. All operating costs are funded internally, so 100% of your tax-deductible donation directly supports victims of human trafficking. No funds are ever paid to the masters to release trafficked victims.

If you would like to make a donation in honor or memory of someone, please include the recipient's name, e-mail or mailing address, and message. A letter will be sent to the recipient to notify them of your gift.
